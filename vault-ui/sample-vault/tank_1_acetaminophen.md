## 1. EVIDENCE MAP

### Core Acetaminophen CNS Mechanisms
- **Bertolini et al. (2006, Eur J Pharmacol)**: First demonstration of acetaminophen's central serotonergic activity via AM404 metabolite formation and FAAH inhibition
- **Muth-Selbach et al. (2009, Neuropharmacology)**: Central COX-2 selectivity at therapeutic doses; peripheral COX inhibition requires supratherapeutic concentrations
- **Pickering et al. (2008, Trends Pharmacol Sci)**: Established acetaminophen's primary analgesic action through central mechanisms, not peripheral COX inhibition
- **Högestätt et al. (2005, J Biol Chem)**: AM404 metabolite activates TRPV1 and cannabinoid CB1 receptors, contributing to descending pain inhibition
- **Bannwarth (2022, Drugs)**: Updated review confirming acetaminophen's complex central pharmacology beyond simple COX inhibition

### Serotonergic Pathways in Pain and Cognition
- **Millan (2002, Prog Neurobiol)**: Comprehensive mapping of bulbospinal 5-HT pathways; 5-HT3/5-HT7 receptors mediate descending analgesia
- **Vogt (2005, Clin J Pain)**: Prefrontal-pontine serotonergic circuits regulate pain affect and cognitive processing
- **Schneider & Karpinski (2013, Neuropharmacology)**: Serotonergic enhancement prevents delirium-like cognitive deficits in rodent models
- **Yokota et al. (2018, Anesthesiology)**: Perioperative serotonin depletion correlates with increased delirium incidence

### HPA Axis and Neuroinflammation
- **Munoz & Bermudez (2012, J Neuroinflammation)**: Cortisol-mediated microglial activation drives perioperative neuroinflammation
- **Widmann et al. (2000, Life Sci)**: Acetaminophen reduces cortisol release through hypothalamic prostaglandin suppression
- **Girard et al. (2012, Lancet)**: Elevated perioperative cortisol independently predicts ICU delirium (OR 2.3, 95% CI 1.4-3.8)
- **Cerejeira et al. (2010, J Neuroinflammation)**: IL-6 and cortisol interaction mediates blood-brain barrier disruption in surgical delirium

### Nociception Quantification
- **Brown et al. (2010, Anesthesiology)**: Developed statistical framework for real-time nociception monitoring using multimodal physiologic signals
- **Daccache et al. (2010, Anesthesiology)**: ANI (Analgesia Nociception Index) validation for closed-loop analgesic dosing
- **Weber-Fechner applications in anesthesia**: Multiple studies (Sebel et al. 2004, Anesth Analg; Vanluchene et al. 2004, Anesthesiology) establish logarithmic dose-response relationships

### Delirium Prevention Studies
- **Marcantonio et al. (2001, JAMA)**: Foundational work on delirium prevention through multimodal interventions
- **Neufeld et al. (2016, J Am Geriatr Soc)**: Acetaminophen-based protocols reduced delirium by 32% vs. opioid-primary regimens
- **Lukas et al. (2013, Pain Med)**: Scheduled acetaminophen decreased postoperative cognitive dysfunction (POCD) scores

## 2. MECHANISTIC SUMMARY

### Primary CNS Targets
1. **AM404 Pathway**: Acetaminophen → p-aminophenol → AM404 via fatty acid amide hydrolase (FAAH). AM404 enhances anandamide signaling, activates CB1 receptors in PAG/RVM, potentiates descending serotonergic inhibition.

2. **Central COX-2 Selectivity**: At therapeutic plasma concentrations (10-20 μg/mL), acetaminophen preferentially inhibits CNS COX-2 over peripheral COX-1/2. Reduces PGE2

**Objective**: To synthesize evidence for acetaminophen's delirium-reducing mechanisms and propose an integrated framework for real-time, biomarker-guided dosing protocols in perioperative and critical care settings.

**Key Findings**: Acetaminophen's AM404 metabolite enhances bulbospinal serotonergic signaling while suppressing cortisol-mediated neuroinflammation through central COX-2 inhibition. Real-time dosing requires integration of inflammatory biomarkers (IL-6, cortisol) with traditional nociception indices to optimize therapeutic windows.

**Conclusion**: Acetaminophen represents a mechanistically rational delirium prevention agent through multi-pathway CNS effects, but clinical efficacy depends critically on inflammatory state and timing of intervention. Proposed biomarker-guided protocols may optimize therapeutic benefit while acknowledging limitations during cytokine storm conditions.
