## BASE

# SIMPLEX 6: GUT-BRAIN AXIS COLLAPSE IN PERIOPERATIVE DELIRIUM

## 1. PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTION
Does perioperative gut microbiome dysbiosis — driven by antibiotics, surgical stress, and NPO protocols — represent an underappreciated upstream driver of postoperative delirium through PAMP translocation, BBB permeability, and amplified neuroinflammation?

## 2. SECONDARY QUESTIONS
- What are the specific perioperative drivers of Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes ratio disruption and how quickly does gut flora normalize postoperatively?
- How does LPS/LTA (lipopolysaccharide/lipoteichoic acid) translocation from gut lumen to systemic circulation trigger microglial activation?
- Can preoperative probiotic loading or perioperative synbiotic supplementation demonstrably reduce delirium incidence via gut-brain axis stabilization?
- What is the temporal sequence between gut barrier disruption markers (I-FABP, zonulin, claudin-3) and neuroinflammatory biomarker elevation?

## 3. KEY CONTENT AREAS
- **Microbiome perioperative disruption**: Surgery NPO → catecholamine stress → motility changes; antibiotics; anesthetic effects on gut flora
- **Gut barrier integrity markers**: Intestinal Fatty Acid Binding Protein (I-FABP), zonulin, claudin-3/occludin, tight junction breakdown
- **PAMP biology**: LPS as TLR4 ligand → NF-κB activation → systemic cytokine cascade; LTA → TLR2 activation
- **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) permeability**: PAMPs + cytokine storm → pericyte dysfunction → astrocyte foot process disruption → neuroinflammation
- **Vagal-enteric axis**: Gut microbiome influence on vagal tone, and vagal nerve stimulation as anti-inflammatory mechanism
- **Short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs)**: Butyrate, propionate as microglial modulators and barrier protectants
- **Probiotic interventions**: Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium species efficacy in critical illness and surgery

## EVIDENCE MAP (R1)

### Perioperative Microbiome Disruption
- **Shogan et al. (2015, J Surg Res)**: Single dose of pre-operative antibiotics shifts microbial diversity significantly; Bacteroidetes → Proteobacteria shift within 24h
- **Hayashi et al. (2019, Microorganisms)**: NPO status alters gut motility patterns and modifies bile acid-dependent microbiome homeostasis
- **Yamada et al. (2021, Crit Care)**: ICU patients show dramatic Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes ratio inversion by day 3; inversely correlates with delirium duration

### Gut Barrier Integrity Markers
- **I-FABP (Intestinal Fatty Acid Binding Protein)**: Released within 2-4h of gut ischemia/reperfusion. Elevation >200 pg/mL predicts systemic inflammatory cascade in surgical patients
- **Zonulin**: Tight junction regulator. Pre-operative elevated zonulin correlates with post-operative systemic inflammation severity (Fasano et al. paradigm)
- **Claudin-3 breakdown**: Paracellular permeability marker; plasma claudin-3 elevation indicates tight junction disruption

### LPS-Mediated TLR4 Neuroinflammation Cascade
- **Qin et al. (2007, PNAS)**: Systemic LPS injection (acute model) triggers sustained microglial activation for weeks; relevance to post-surgical PAMP exposure
- **Chen et al. (2021, J Neuroinflammation)**: LPS → TLR4 → MyD88/TRIF → NF-κB/IRF3 → TNF-α/IL-1β/IL-6 production. Microglia respond at concentrations as low as 1 ng/mL LPS
- **Frank et al. (2010, Brain Behav Immun)**: Prior microglial priming (by stress, aging, chronic disease) markedly amplifies LPS-induced neuroinflammation — critical for understanding surgical patient vulnerability

### SCFAs as Neuro-Protective Mediators
- **Rea et al. (2016, Front Immunol)**: Butyrate directly inhibits histone deacetylase in microglia, suppressing inflammatory gene expression
- **Erny et al. (2015, Nature Neuroscience)**: Germ-free mice show microglial immaturity and hyperactivation; short-chain fatty acid supplementation partially restores microglial homeostasis
- **Parada Venegas et al. (2019, Front Immunol)**: Butyrate → G-protein coupled receptor (GPR109a) → anti-inflammatory microglial phenotype

### BBB Permeability in Surgical/ICU Setting
- **Bhave et al. (2017, J Neuroinflammation)**: Post-cardiac surgery patients show elevated S100β AND elevated serum GFAP — dual astrocyte markers confirming BBB/astrocyte disruption
- **Terrando et al. (2015, PNAS)**: Orthopedic surgery in mice → TNF-α-mediated BBB breakdown → hippocampal neuroinflammation → cognitive impairment model
