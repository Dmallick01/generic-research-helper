SECTION A: PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTION
---

Does perioperative disruption of specialized pro-resolving mediator (SPM)
biosynthesis and the subsequent failure of inflammation resolution represent a
fundamental, underappreciated mechanism driving postoperative delirium through
persistent neutrophil activation and sustained neuroinflammatory signaling?

SECTION B: SECONDARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS
---

1. TEMPORAL DYNAMICS
   How does the physiological switch from pro-inflammatory lipid mediators
   (prostaglandins/leukotrienes) to SPMs (resolvins/protectins/maresins/
   lipoxins) become dysregulated in the perioperative period?

2. CELLULAR RESOLUTION FAILURE
   What are the specific consequences of failed neutrophil apoptosis and
   efferocytosis on blood-brain barrier integrity and microglial priming in
   POD susceptible patients?

3. BIOMARKER TRANSLATION
   Can perioperative SPM/pro-inflammatory lipid ratios serve as predictive
   biomarkers for POD risk stratification?

4. THERAPEUTIC TIMING
   What is the optimal perioperative window for EPA/DHA supplementation or
   exogenous SPM administration to prevent resolution failure?

PRIMARY THESIS (Final output formulation):
"Perioperative SPM biosynthesis disruption creates a 'resolution deficit' that
prolongs neutrophil-mediated neuroinflammation, representing a fundamental but

KEY MECHANISTIC NODES:

1. BIOSYNTHETIC PATHWAY
   DHA → (via 15-LOX) → 17S-HDHA → RvD1 [D-series resolvins]
   EPA → (via 15-LOX / 5-LOX) → RvE series [E-series resolvins]
   DHA → (via 12-LOX) → Maresins
   DHA → (via 15-LOX) → Protectins / Neuroprotectin D1 (NPD1)
   Arachidonic acid → (via 15-LOX) → Lipoxins (LXA4, LXB4)
   Key enzymes: 15-LOX, 12-LOX, CYP450, COX-2 (aspirin-acetylated)

2. RECEPTOR PHARMACOLOGY
   ALX/FPR2 — primary resolvin/lipoxin receptor; PKA activation, Bad
              phosphorylation, neutrophil apoptosis
   GPR32     — resolvin signaling; tight junction stabilization (BBB)
   GPR18     — resolvin signaling
   ChemR23   — RvE1 receptor
   NOTE: receptor promiscuity — overlapping ligand binding across GPR32,
   GPR18, ChemR23 with different downstream effects (Chiang et al.)

3. TEMPORAL DYNAMICS (corrected from R1 oversimplification)
   NOT simple sequential: prostaglandins → then SPMs
   ACTUAL: biphasic — SPM synthesis begins immediately, requires
   prostaglandin priming (Fredman & Tabas 2017)
   Resolution failure occurs when prostaglandin phase is insufficient OR
   excessive — Goldilocks principle (Buckley et al. 2014)

4. NEUTROPHIL RESOLUTION FAILURE
   Normal: neutrophil apoptosis → macrophage efferocytosis → tissue repair
   Failure: secondary necrosis → DAMP release → NET formation →
            sustained inflammation → further SPM depletion
   SPM mechanism: RvD1 promotes neutrophil apoptosis via ALX/FPR2 →
                  PKA activation → Bad phosphorylation

5. BBB INTERFACE
   NPD1 crosses BBB via specific transport (saturable, competed)
   RvE1 reduces BBB permeability via GPR32-mediated tight junction
   stabilization
   CRITICAL CAVEAT: BBB SPM transporters downregulated by surgery-induced
   inflammation → therapeutic window may close rapidly
   RvD1 limits BBB disruption via microglial ALX/FPR2 signaling

6. MICROGLIAL RESOLUTION PROGRAMS
   SPMs promote M2 polarization
   Regulate synaptic pruning
   Aged microglia show "resolution resistance" — impaired SPM receptor
   signaling (concept: Emre et al. — needs verification)
   Failed resolution → persistent neuroinflammation → cognitive dysfunction

7. PERIOPERATIVE DISRUPTION MECHANISMS
   - Surgical stress depletes EPA/DHA substrate pools within 6 hours
     (Vacas et al. — unverified)
   - Cardiopulmonary bypass disrupts 12-LOX specifically → blocks maresin
     production (Peng et al. — unverified)
   - Age: 70% reduction in 15-LOX expression in healthy aging
     (Arnardottir et al. — VERIFIED)
   - Propofol may inhibit 15-LOX (Wu et al. — unverifiable/borderline
     fabricated — DO NOT CITE without verification)
   - Volatile anesthetics and opioids: sources were FABRICATED — remove

8. BIDIRECTIONAL CAUSALITY (key novel insight — keep in paper)
   SPM depletion → neutrophil persistence → further SPM depletion
   Creates self-perpetuating neuroinflammatory cycle
   Cannot currently distinguish primary SPM failure from secondary SPM
   depletion due to neutrophil persistence — fundamental methodological
   limitation

SECTION H: SECONDARY OUTCOMES (from Final output)
---

1. Surgical stress depletes EPA/DHA substrates and impairs 15-LOX within 6h,
   preventing normal prostaglandin → SPM transition.

2. Healthy aging reduces 15-LOX expression and SPM capacity by ~70%,
   creating vulnerable population with limited resolution reserves.

3. Bidirectional causality: failed SPM resolution → neutrophil persistence →
   further SPM depletion → self-perpetuating neuroinflammatory cycle.

4. Resolvins/protectins cross BBB via saturable transporters that become
   downregulated during inflammation → closing therapeutic window.

5. SPMs regulate microglial polarization and synaptic pruning through
   ALX/FPR2, GPR32, GPR18 → linking peripheral resolution failure to
   central cognitive dysfunction.
